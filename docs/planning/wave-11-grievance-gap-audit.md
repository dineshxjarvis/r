# Wave 11 — Grievance and Complaint Gap Audit

## A. Outcome and boundary

Anyone in the required worker, contractor or community audiences can safely raise an issue, receive a receipt, reach the correct accountable authority, observe a safe status, obtain a reasoned response and use feedback/appeal—without exposing protected identity or pretending Strata replaces specialized statutory systems.

## B. Actors and accountability

Reporter/affected person/representative; assisted-intake officer; triage/nodal grievance officer; dealing/redress officer; action owner; competent-authority/quality reviewer; appellate officer; privacy/vigilance/POSH/safeguarding authority; external-system reconciliation operator; auditor. Titles are policy data mapped to posts, mandates and jurisdiction.

## C. Sources and outputs

Sources include web/mobile/kiosk/telephone/mail, external portals, evidence, domain records and effective legal/route policies. Outputs include receipt/status, case/action/disposition/appeal, protected access receipts, external reconciliation, aggregate patterns and cross-domain safety/corrective work.

## D. Lifecycles and clocks

Intake, case, transfer, assignment, action, response, disposition, feedback, appeal, protection and external mirror remain separate. Acknowledgement, assessment, acceptance, interim, disposal and appeal clocks use effective rules; no silent pause.

## E. Authorization and separation

Case read != reporter identity; triage != disposition; dealing != required quality review; original decision != appeal; implicated chain != protected-case owner; platform/index operator != content access. All actions resolve current post/appointment/mandate/jurisdiction and conflict.

## F. Cross-domain consistency

Safety content triggers incident/observation/inspection without awaiting grievance classification. Grievance disposition cannot forge defect/CAPA verification. Specialized cases expose only safe handoff metadata. Search/dashboards exclude protected content and suppress small cohorts.

## G. Failure and adversarial scenarios

Anonymous intake, spam, missing mine, shared phone, unsafe contact, implicated manager, absent handler, unaccepted transfer, appointment expiry, duplicate CPGRAMS import, external outage, lost receipt, malicious attachment, mistranslation, partial response, false closure, retaliation, appeal, search inference and small-cohort analytics were challenged.

## H. External and degraded operation

CPGRAMS/specialized authority remains authoritative where policy says so. Submission/acknowledgement/disposal are separate. Physical/telephone/mail routes continue during outage; reconciliation preserves real receipt time and proof without backdating.

## I. Gap dispositions

### GAP-11-001

- **Gap:** production requirement demanded low-barrier intake while prototype prose prohibited it.
- **Impact:** PS §4.8 remained missing and users had no unified receipt/status.
- **Resolution:** governed front door with per-type native/federated/assisted/information modes supersedes prototype exclusion.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-002

- **Gap:** a general queue could expose POSH, vigilance, reporter identity or retaliation content to implicated management.
- **Impact:** harm, confidentiality breach and suppression.
- **Resolution:** early safety/special-regime screen, reporter vault, independent protected routes and purpose-logged identity disclosure.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-003

- **Gap:** receipt, case, transfer, action, disposition and closure could collapse into one status.
- **Impact:** forwarding or replying could falsely appear as redress.
- **Resolution:** independent evidence-bearing lifecycles and closure gates.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-004

- **Gap:** title/enums could route to a named “grievance officer” without current appointment, jurisdiction, conflict or fallback.
- **Impact:** cases stall or reach an accused/unauthorized person.
- **Resolution:** post/mandate/jurisdiction/conflict-based routing, acceptance and vacancy escalation.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-005

- **Gap:** safety complaints and corrective work could close independently.
- **Impact:** complainant receives disposal while hazard remains hidden/unowned.
- **Resolution:** immediate domain handoff, typed links, action truth and no false verification claim.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-006

- **Gap:** complainant status, communication recovery, feedback and independent appeal were undefined.
- **Impact:** low trust, privacy leaks and overwritten decisions.
- **Resolution:** safe receipt credential, neutral communications, immutable feedback/disposition and separate appeal route.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-007

- **Gap:** aggregate pattern analytics could expose protected/small cohorts or convert allegations into adverse decisions.
- **Impact:** re-identification, retaliation and unfair action.
- **Resolution:** minimum cohorts/suppression, authorized manifests, provenance and human-reviewed non-adverse signals.
- **Status:** `RESOLVED`.

### GAP-11-008

- **Gap:** exact operator/authority case catalogue, routes, clocks, POSH/PIDPI handling, external schemas and retention require competent approval.
- **Impact:** architecture cannot certify legal handling or live interoperability nationally.
- **Resolution:** effective policy boundary fixed; Ministry/operator legal, HR, vigilance, POSH, privacy and records owners approve contents; Wave 12/onboarding supplies adapters.
- **Status:** `ACCEPTED_RISK`.

## J. Decisions requiring human approval

1. Ministry/operator owners publish case types, authoritative modes, eligibility, clocks, routes and appeal hierarchy.
2. POSH/vigilance/industrial-relations owners approve pre-intake guidance, safe handoff and permissible metadata.
3. Privacy/records owners approve identity disclosure, safe contact, retention, legal hold and aggregate thresholds.
4. Domain owners approve cross-domain creation/linking and what remediation state may appear to reporters.

## K. Canonical documents that must change

Feature, logical model, API/indexes, authorization, glossary, capability/inventory, solution-context conflict, decisions and tracker.

## L. Exit verdict

**Pre-design verdict: FAIL.** Only a prototype closure-link idea existed and directly conflicted with required production intake.

**Post-design adversarial verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS.** GAP-11-001 through 007 are resolved. GAP-11-008 remains an explicit authority/onboarding/Wave 12 dependency.
