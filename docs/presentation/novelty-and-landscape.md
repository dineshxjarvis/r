# Strata — Novelty and Existing-System Landscape

## 1. The claim to avoid

Do not say:

> “There is currently no centralized coal safety or clearance portal.”

That is false. SWCS/PRIMS, PARIVESH and NCMSR already cover important parts of the domain.

## 2. The defensible novelty

> Strata is a unified Ministry portal whose differentiator is clause-to-closure governance: it turns authoritative documents and portal events into mine-specific obligations, field-verifiable evidence, corrective workflows, authority-controlled closure and traceable portfolio decisions.

## 4. Five novelty pillars

### 4.1 Clause-to-closure traceability

Most systems store a document or submission. Strata preserves the graph from source clause to obligation, dated work, evidence, finding, action and closure.

### 4.2 Evidence integrity

A file attachment is not automatically proof. Strata treats location accuracy, capture path, target asset, time uncertainty and device signals as part of the evidence verdict.

### 4.3 Governance-aware authorisation

Permissions come from time-bounded appointments at specific assets. Transfers and expiry affect authority automatically; regulator-raised findings remain structurally outside operator closure authority.

### 4.4 Process-integrity analytics

Strata examines not just hazards but the compliance process: suspiciously fast closures, repeated extensions, zero-rejection verifiers, repeated recurrence and excessive evidence overrides.

### 4.5 Honest uncertainty

The system distinguishes:

- submitted from independently satisfied;
- missing evidence from confirmed failure;
- unverified from suspect evidence;
- missing data from zero; and
- an offline time window from a falsely precise timestamp.

## 5. Why the unified portal is still novel

The novelty is not placing links to portals on one homepage. A true unified portal provides:

- shared identity and appointments;
- one mine/asset master;
- reusable data rather than re-entry;
- common document/evidence provenance;
- one obligation and workflow model;
- common audit semantics;
- Ministry portfolio visibility; and
- phased native migration of duplicated Ministry-owned functions.

## 6. Why Strata can supersede SWCS but federates other systems

SWCS is a Ministry of Coal platform, so the same institutional owner can choose to migrate its functions after parity. PARIVESH and DGMS belong to different ministries/statutory authorities. Strata may unify their user journey and exchange data, but it cannot silently take over their legal authority.

## 7. Official landscape evidence

- The Ministry describes SWCS as the gateway for approvals needed to operationalize coal mines and confirms PRIMS production/project functions: [Ministry of Coal SWCS](https://www.coal.gov.in/nominated-authority/single-window-system).
- PARIVESH is the MoEFCC workflow for Environmental, Forest, Wildlife and CRZ clearances: [Official PARIVESH](https://parivesh.nic.in/).
- The Ministry's annual report describes NCMSR's Accident and Safety Audit modules: [Ministry of Coal Annual Report](https://www.coal.nic.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/chap14AnnualReport2025en2.pdf).

## 8. One-line comparisons for Q&A

- **Versus SWCS:** SWCS operationalizes mines; Strata governs ongoing clause-to-closure compliance and is the proposed successor user experience.
- **Versus PARIVESH:** PARIVESH grants and monitors green clearances; Strata operationalizes each condition inside the mine while preserving PARIVESH as statutory authority.
- **Versus NCMSR:** NCMSR captures accidents and safety audits; Strata connects them to obligations, field evidence, recurring defects and CAPA across domains.
- **Versus a BI dashboard:** BI visualizes submitted data; Strata controls how the underlying fact is created, verified, escalated and traced.
- **Versus a document chatbot:** a chatbot answers questions; Strata creates governed work with deadlines, authority and evidence.
